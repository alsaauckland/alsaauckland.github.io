Five distinct opportunities.

ALSA's 2026 programme is structured into five partnership streams, each designed to deliver value in different ways. Firms can sponsor individual streams or commit to the full programme.

Stream 1
Academic Skills & Student Support

Workshops, drop-in sessions, the ALSA Legal Writing Competition judged by a High Court Judge's Clerk, and cultural competence workshops preparing students to navigate cultural difference with intelligence and sensitivity. Named partner across all Stream 1 activity.

Stream 2
Professional Development

Networking workshops, specialist practice panels, coffee networking, and firm-led masterclasses. Priority firm representation at every event.

Stream 3
Student Engagement

Social events, the Inaugural Launch Event, monthly gatherings, and the Closing Night in October. Your brand present at the community moments that define the ALSA experience across the year.

Stream 4
Mentoring & Community

A focused four-month programme in Semester 2 designed to deliver maximum impact. Your lawyers mentor directly, your firm builds sustained relationships.

Stream 5
Digital Content & Media

Short-form video content, the ALSA podcast (launching Semester 2), and the monthly newsletter. Partner acknowledgement reaches audiences well beyond event attendees.
