Workshop · Cultural Competency

Practising across cultures.

Cultural competency is a skill the profession increasingly expects and rarely teaches. This workshop, the Chinese edition of our cultural competency series, gives you a practical grounding in working with clients and colleagues across cultures. Fifty places, open to all law students.

Why come

The skill the profession assumes you have.

Legal work happens across cultures every day, in the client you are advising, the colleague you are working beside, and the community you are serving. Getting it right builds trust. Getting it wrong quietly costs you. This session gives you a practical grounding most students never receive, starting with the Chinese community and the contexts you are most likely to meet in practice.

Practical, not theoretical.

Real situations and how to handle them well, not a lecture on definitions. You leave with things you can actually use.

Relevant from your first clerkship.

Cultural competency shows up early. Knowing how to navigate it sets you apart before you have even graduated.

Open to all law students.

You do not need to be an ALSA member to come. If this matters to you, you belong in the room.
