The Process

What happens after you apply.

So you know exactly what you are signing up for.

01
Apply
Submit this application. Applications close at 11:59 pm on 10 July 2026.
02
Review
We review applications the week of 13 July. Shortlisted candidates receive a task via email.
03
Task
A substantive brief relevant to your role. We want to see how you plan and execute.
04
Interview
45 minutes with two executive members, including a structured debrief on your task. 16 to 18 July.
05
Decision
All candidates hear back the week of 20 July, whether successful or not.
06
Onboarding and Team Vote
Successful candidates begin a two-week onboarding period. At the end, cabinet votes to confirm your place. We assess fit, and you decide if this is right for you.
