Written Questions

Write about what you actually did, not what you generally do. Include the decision you made and the result. "I helped organise events" tells us nothing. "Sign-ups had stalled at 20, so I rewrote the event post and personally messaged 30 people, and we finished with 75 in the room" tells us everything. Everything you write here can come up at interview, so keep it honest. For more on how to make your application stand out, read the helpful information.

The Process

What happens after you apply.

So you know exactly what you are signing up for. The full timeline is on the helpful information page.

01
Apply
Submit this application. Applications close at 9:00 pm on Sunday 20 September.
02
Review
Cabinet reviews every application on 20 to 21 September. Shortlisted applicants receive a short task by email.
03
Task
A short task relevant to the role, handed out on 22 September and due within a day or two. It fits around study, around three hours of work in total.
04
Interview, round one
Interviews run from 26 September. This is the part the helpful information guide prepares you for.
05
Interview, round two
In the days that follow, shortlisted applicants progress to a second, final round with the team.
06
Final selections
Offers go out and the new team is confirmed by 3 October. Everyone who interviewed hears back either way.
