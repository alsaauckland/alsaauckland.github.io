Questions

Before you apply.

Can I apply for more than one role?
Interns can pick up to three roles and rank them by preference. Officer applicants apply for the one open officer role. If you are open to either track, the intern roles are the better entry point this intake.
Do I need prior experience?
No. We value how you think, whether you follow through, and how you work with a team, not the length of your CV. The questions and the task are built for you to display these qualities, whatever your background.
Can first-years and international students apply?
Yes. Every role is open to any law student at the University of Auckland or AUT, at any stage of your degree.
How much time does it take?
It depends on the role, and each role page lists its own estimate. Most sit between a few hours a week and 8 to 10 hours, with more during event weeks. We would rather you were honest about your capacity than overcommit.
What happens after I submit?
You will see the full timeline on the application form: review, an interview, and a decision the week of 20 July, with onboarding beginning with Semester Two. Officers also complete a short task; interns are assessed on their written answers.
