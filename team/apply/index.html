How it works

The application process.

A look at what applying involves, so there are no surprises when the 2027 round opens. The process is deliberately thorough, and it differs slightly by track.

01
Find your fit
Read the role descriptions above and the executive standards below to work out where you belong: intern, officer, or cabinet.
02
Apply when applications open
Officers complete a CV, a short video, written questions, and a role-relevant task. Interns complete a CV and written questions, with no task or video, because the intern tier is about potential, not experience.
03
Interview
Shortlisted candidates meet the team for an interview so we can see how you think and how you would work with us.
04
Two-week onboarding
Every new exec member starts with a two-week onboarding period, paired with an onboarding partner. It is mutual: we assess whether you can operate at the standard, and you decide whether this is right for you.
05
Confirmation
At the end of the two weeks, cabinet votes to confirm your place. This is how we stay accountable for who is on the team.

Before you apply, read the standard we hold ourselves to. It is the clearest picture of what being on the ALSA exec actually asks of you.
