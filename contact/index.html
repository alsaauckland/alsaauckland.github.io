Contact ALSA.

Several ways to reach us, depending on what you need.

Whether you are a student looking to get involved, a firm interested in partnership, or anyone else wanting to reach us, here is how to make contact.

General Inquiries

Fill in the form below and your message will be sent directly to the ALSA team. We aim to respond within three working days. You can also reach us directly at Enquiries@alsaauckland.org.

Students: use Membership Question or Events and Programmes. Firms and practitioners: use Collaboration, Sponsorship, or Media. Your message will reach the right person automatically.

Social Media

For the latest updates, event photos, and day to day ALSA happenings, find us on:

Instagram · @alsa.akl LinkedIn · alsa-akl

ALSA Community

The ALSA Discord is where members stay in the loop between events. Join to get updates on upcoming events, connect with other members, and hear from the team directly.
